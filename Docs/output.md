# Script Presentasi Bahasa Inggris: VelaPDF

Berikut adalah rancangan (script) presentasi Bahasa Inggris untuk aplikasi **VelaPDF**, disusun untuk durasi sekitar 3-5 menit sesuai dengan struktur yang dianjurkan.

## 1. Opening (Pembukaan)

**Greeting & Perkenalan Diri:**
"Good morning, everyone. / Good afternoon, everyone."
"My name is Yogi Ario Pratama, from class 3B. I hope you are all having a great day."

**Menyampaikan tujuan presentasi:**
"Today, I am here to present a mobile application that I have been working on. The application is called VelaPDF, a powerful yet simple tool designed to make your document management much easier."

## 2. Main Material (Isi)

**Menjelaskan topik/produk:**
"As we know, in this digital era, we deal with digital documents every single day. That is why I created VelaPDF. VelaPDF is a mobile application that focuses on handling and managing PDF files directly from your smartphone."

**Menjelaskan fungsi/manfaat:**
"Our application offers several essential features:
First, you can merge multiple PDF files into one single document. 
Second, it has a built-in image-to-PDF converter, which allows you to turn your photos or scanned documents into a PDF file instantly. 
And third, we provide a PDF compression feature, so you can reduce the file size of your documents without losing much quality."

**Menjelaskan kelebihan:**
"What makes VelaPDF stand out from other similar apps? VelaPDF is designed with a very clean and user-friendly interface. It is lightweight, fast, and most importantly, it can perform all these tasks locally on your device without needing an internet connection. This ensures your private documents remain highly secure."

**Memberikan contoh atau ilustrasi:**
"For example, imagine you are a student, just like me. You need to submit an assignment consisting of several scanned pages and some downloaded PDF materials. Instead of opening a laptop or using a paid online service, you can simply open VelaPDF, select your images and PDFs, merge them, compress them if the file is too large, and send it to your lecturer right away. It saves a lot of time and effort."

## 3. Closing (Penutup)

**Menyimpulkan isi presentasi:**
"To summarize, VelaPDF is a comprehensive, offline-first mobile application that simplifies how you create, merge, and compress PDF documents on the go. It is practical, secure, and very useful for students, workers, and everyone in between."

**Memberikan kesimpulan akhir:**
"I believe that an app like VelaPDF is not just a tool, but a daily necessity that enhances our productivity. Thank you very much for your time and attention. Have a wonderful day!"

---

## 📌 Tips Tambahan Saat Presentasi:
1. **Pronunciation & Intonasi:** Berlatihlah membacanya dengan suara lantang. Berikan penekanan (stressing) pada kata-kata kunci seperti *merge*, *compress*, *secure*, dan *offline*.
2. **Body Language (Bahasa Tubuh):** Jangan terus-menerus melihat ke arah teks presentasi. Buat kontak mata dengan audiens atau dosen terutama saat memberikan contoh (ilustrasi).
3. **Kecepatan Bicara (Pacing):** Baca dengan tempo yang tenang dan santai. Tidak perlu terlalu terburu-buru agar durasi 3-5 menit dapat tercapai dengan pas dan pesan dapat ditangkap dengan jelas.
